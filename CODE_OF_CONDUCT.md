# Code of conduct

BTS Office is built by and for students, teachers and volunteers of every background and skill level. To keep it a good place to learn and contribute:

* Be kind and patient, especially with people asking their first question.
* Criticise ideas and code, never people.
* No harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or language, in any language.
* Respect privacy: don't post other people's personal details or files without permission.
* Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem privately, email **btspl99@gmail.com**.
