# Microsoft Store listing — BTS Office (English, en-US)

## Description (max 10,000 chars)
BTS Office is a free, open-source office suite made for students. Write assignments, calculate marks, present projects and read PDFs. It opens and saves .docx, .xlsx and .pptx files, so you can work with what your school or college sends you. No account, no ads, no fee.

FOUR APPS IN ONE
• BTS Docs, a word processor with real A4 pages, styles and style sets, tables, pictures, headers and footers, table of contents, track changes, comments, citations and bibliography (APA, MLA, IEEE, Harvard, Chicago), captions and mail merge for certificates and letters.
• BTS Sheets, a spreadsheet with 400+ formulas, PivotTables, charts, sparklines, conditional formatting, Goal Seek, Flash Fill, sort and filter, data validation and sheet protection, with rupee number formats.
• BTS Slides, a presentation app with 31 themes, Slide Master, shapes, icons, tables and charts, transitions and animations, a presenter view with notes and timer, rehearse timings and handout PDFs.
• BTS PDF, a reader with thumbnails, bookmarks, search, highlighting, drawing and sticky notes that you can save into the PDF.

MADE FOR STUDENTS IN INDIA AND EVERYWHERE
• 50 professional fonts built in, including Hindi and Marathi fonts, so your documents look the same on every computer.
• 27 ready-made templates: resume, assignment cover page, research paper, certificate, marks sheet, attendance tracker, budget, class presentation, science fair and more.
• Works fully offline. Your files never leave your computer.
• Light and dark themes, familiar keyboard shortcuts, and Alt+Q to find any command by name.
• Version history keeps earlier copies of your files every time you save.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
BTS Office is released under the GNU GPL v3 and developed by Bhoomiputra Tech Solutions Private Limited. Source code: https://github.com/btspl99/bts-office
