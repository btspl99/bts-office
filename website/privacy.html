BTS Office

Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

BTS Office is a free, open-source office suite developed by Bhoomiputra Tech Solutions Private Limited (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the BTS Office desktop app and this website.

Your files stay on your computer

Documents, spreadsheets, presentations and PDFs you open or create are read and saved only on your own computer. We never upload, see or store your files. BTS Office has no account, no sign-in and no cloud storage.

What the app sends over the internet

  • Update checks. Copies installed from office.btspl.tech check https://office.btspl.tech/updates/latest.yml for new versions and download them. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address and app version to our web host. No personal data or file content is sent. You can turn this off in File > Options & about. Copies installed from Microsoft Store are updated by the Store instead and do not contact our server.
  • Links you click open in your web browser, under that website's own privacy policy.

BTS Office contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or telemetry.

Information stored on your computer

To make the app work, BTS Office keeps a list of recent files, your settings (such as theme and the name you enter for authorship), automatic recovery copies of unsaved work and, if enabled, earlier versions of files you save. All of this stays in your user profile on your computer and you can delete it at any time.

This website

office.btspl.tech is a static website hosted by Hostinger. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Hostinger keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operation.

Children

BTS Office is made for students of all ages. Because we collect no personal information, it is safe for use in schools.
