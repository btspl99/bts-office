Four apps, one download

Always up to date, never in the way

When we publish a new version, BTS Office downloads it quietly while you work. It installs the next time you close the app, or straight away if you choose Restart now. If you have unsaved work, it asks you to save first.

  • Checks automaticallyA few seconds after you open it, and every few hours after that.
  • Verified before installingEvery download is checked against its SHA-512 fingerprint. A damaged file is thrown away.
  • Lab-friendlyComputer labs can turn automatic updates off, or point every PC at their own mirror.

Help

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

Windows shows this for new apps that aren’t yet code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only need to do this once. If you want to check the file, its SHA-512 fingerprint is listed in updates/latest.yml.

Will it open the Word, Excel and PowerPoint files my college sends?

Yes. BTS Office opens and saves .docx, .xlsx and .pptx. Most files look the same; very complex layouts, macros and some advanced features may look slightly different.

Is it really free?

Yes, for everyone, forever. There are no ads, no account and no paid version. It’s open source, so it will stay free.

Do I need the internet?

Only to download it and to receive updates. Everything else works offline, and your files stay on your computer.

How do I update?

You don’t need to do anything. BTS Office updates itself. To check right away, open any app and go to File > Options & about > Check for updates.

We run a computer lab. Can we control updates?

Yes. Untick Download and install updates automatically in File > Options & about, or add "autoUpdate": false to %APPDATA%\BTS Office\settings.json on each PC. To serve updates from your own server, add "updateUrl": "https://your-server/bts-office/" and copy our updates folder there.

How does voice typing work without internet?

The first time you use voice typing (Alt+Shift+D, or the microphone button in the title bar), BTS Office downloads a speech model for your language once (about 40 MB for English or Hindi). After that it works offline, and what you say never leaves your computer. You can remove the models in File > Options & about.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit, with at least 4 GB of RAM and 500 MB of free disk space.
